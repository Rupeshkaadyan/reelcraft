# Reelcraft privacy policy

**Last updated: 18 October 2026**

Reelcraft is a video editor for iPhone. It is built so that your footage, your projects and your data never leave your device.

## What we collect

**Nothing.** Reelcraft has no account system, no analytics, no advertising SDK, no crash reporter and no server. We do not collect personal data, device identifiers, usage data or location.

## Where your content lives

- Imported clips, thumbnails, projects, ideas and export history are stored in Reelcraft's private app storage on your iPhone.
- Exported videos are written to the app's temporary folder and then saved to your photo library only when you tap Save.
- Deleting a project in the app deletes its media from app storage. Deleting the app removes everything Reelcraft stored.

## Permissions and why they are used

| Permission | Why | What happens |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Photo library (read) | So you can choose clips to edit | Only the videos you pick are copied into the app |
| Photo library (add) | So finished videos can be saved | Only the exports you confirm are added |
| Camera | To record clips inside the app | Recording stays on the device |
| Microphone | To record audio with those clips | Audio is written into the clip file on the device |
| Speech recognition | To generate captions | Apple's speech framework runs on device when the model is available; Reelcraft does not upload audio |

You can change any of these in iOS Settings → Reelcraft.

## Third parties

Reelcraft does not send data to third parties. It uses Apple system frameworks (AVFoundation, Photos, Speech, Core Image) under Apple's own terms. There are no third-party SDKs in the app.

## Children

Reelcraft does not collect data from anyone, including children.

## Your choices

Because there is no account and no server, there is nothing to request, export or delete remotely. Removing the app removes all app data. Videos you saved to Photos remain in your photo library until you delete them there.

## Changes

If a future version adds a feature that needs data to leave the device (for example optional cloud backup), this policy will be updated before that version ships, and the feature will be opt-in.
