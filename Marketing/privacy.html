REELCRAFT

Privacy policy

Reelcraft edits video on your iPhone. Your footage, projects and data never leave the device.

What we collect

Nothing. No account, no analytics, no advertising SDK, no crash reporter, no server. We do not collect personal data, device identifiers, usage data or location.

Where your content lives

  • Imported clips, thumbnails, projects, ideas and export history live in Reelcraft's private app storage on your iPhone.
  • Exported videos are written to the app's folder and saved to your photo library only when you tap Save.
  • Deleting a project deletes its media from app storage. Deleting the app removes everything Reelcraft stored.

Permissions and why they are used

Change any of these in iOS Settings → Reelcraft.

Third parties

No data goes to third parties. Reelcraft uses Apple system frameworks (AVFoundation, Photos, Speech, Core Image) under Apple's terms, and ships no third-party SDKs.

Children

Reelcraft collects no data from anyone, including children.

Your choices

There is no account and no server, so there is nothing to request, export or delete remotely. Removing the app removes all app data. Videos saved to Photos stay in your library until you delete them there.

Changes

If a future version adds anything that sends data off the device, this policy will be updated before that version ships and the feature will be opt-in.
